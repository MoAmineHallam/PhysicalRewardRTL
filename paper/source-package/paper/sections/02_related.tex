\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Executable RTL generation}

VerilogEval and RTLLM established executable benchmarks for RTL generation
~\cite{liu2023verilogeval,lu2024rtllm}. The revised VerilogEval adds
specification-to-RTL tasks, in-context examples, and failure classification
~\cite{pinckney2025revisiting}. ChipVerilog extends evaluation to larger
OpenCores-derived designs and cross-module interactions~\cite{tan2026chipverilog}.
OpenLLM-RTL expands the RTLLM benchmark and releases verified training data
~\cite{liu2024openllmrtl}. TuRTLe integrates benchmarks to evaluate syntax,
functionality, synthesis, and PPA within a common framework
~\cite{garciagasulla2025turtle}.
VeriGen, RTLCoder, CodeV, and CraftRTL improve generation through specialized
pretraining, supervised adaptation, data construction, or targeted repair
~\cite{thakur2024verigen,liu2024rtlcoder,zhao2024codev,liu2024craftrtl}.
VeriSeek introduces code-structure-guided reinforcement learning, while
VeriReason combines supervised learning, testbench feedback, and group-relative
policy optimization~\cite{wang2024veriseek,wang2025verireason}. VeriPrefer uses
testbench outcomes to construct preference pairs for correctness-oriented
training~\cite{wang2026veriprefer}. FormalRTL
couples executable specifications to formal verification in a multi-agent
synthesis flow~\cite{formalrtl2026}. VeriRefine makes specification refinement
an explicit stage and routes failures back to either interpretation or code
generation~\cite{kong2026verirefine}. These approaches improve or assess
functional generation. Our study asks how physical quality changes across a
policy's sampled outputs, retaining both oracle rejections and implementation
failures in the evaluation.

\subsection{LLM-driven physical optimization}

Physical feedback can guide training, target-specific adaptation, or search.
BetterV uses task-specific generative discriminators to guide Verilog generation
for synthesis and verification objectives~\cite{pei2024betterv}.
RTLRewriter rewrites an existing implementation~\cite{yao2024rtlrewriter}.
SymRTLO combines LLM rewriting with symbolic reasoning and formal/test-based
validation for physical optimization~\cite{wang2025symrtlo}.
PPA-RTL constructs synthesis-scored preference pairs and applies direct
preference optimization (DPO)~\cite{ppartl2025}.  ChipSeek uses curriculum-guided
dynamic policy optimization with syntax, functional, and synthesis-derived
feedback~\cite{chipseek2025}.  Alpha-RTL instead adapts the
policy for each target at test time through an executable EDA loop
~\cite{zhou2026alphartl}.  Dr.~RTL uses commercial-tool feedback, critical-path
analysis, parallel rewriting, and reusable optimization skills
~\cite{drrtl2026}.  POET and EvolVE apply evolutionary search to verified RTL
variants~\cite{poet2026,evolve2026}. REvolution evolves separate populations for
bug fixing and PPA improvement~\cite{min2025revolution}. COEVO jointly searches
correctness and PPA using an adaptive correctness gate and Pareto-based
selection~\cite{coevo2026}. RTL-OPT contributes a benchmark of
suboptimal/human-optimized RTL pairs and an automated correctness/PPA
evaluation flow~\cite{lu2026rtlopt}. Fu et al. assess synthesis quality using
area, delay, and tool warnings, and distinguish single-attempt quality from
best-of-several performance~\cite{fu2026synthesis}. Their evaluation reinforces
the need to specify how sampled outcomes are aggregated; our endpoint uses
post-route FPGA timing and retains each draw's original multiplicity.

Table~\ref{tab:positioning} distinguishes selected implementations from policy
distributions. ChipSeek reports mean and extreme physical metrics among correct
generations; our frozen paired evaluation retains the full draw denominator,
including failures. The perfect-selector analysis separately compares repeated
SFT sampling with one optimized draw. The internal reward controls do not
establish head-to-head superiority over PPA-RTL or ChipSeek.

\subsection{Physical predictors and reward reliability}

Pre-synthesis predictors reduce the cost of FPGA design-space exploration
~\cite{ustun2020accurate,wu2022highlevel,fang2024presynthesis}. Optimizing a
learned reward changes its input distribution and can magnify prediction
errors~\cite{gao2023scaling}. In our method, the predictor guides training,
while post-route and board measurements provide physical evidence. The
historical reward trajectory illustrates why this separation matters, without
implying that physical predictors fail in general. Our contribution is a
controlled evaluation of physical-reward training at a fixed draw budget,
supported by matched reward controls, paired design-level analysis, and
candidate diagnostics. The resulting conclusions apply to the studied
streaming accelerators and sampled oracle contract; they do not establish
formal equivalence or superiority on industrial RTL.
