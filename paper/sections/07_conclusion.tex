\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Correctness-gated, proxy-guided policy optimization improves the physical
utility of a fixed-budget RTL draw within the studied streaming-accelerator
domain.  On the sealed split, the RF reward package raises failure-penalized
post-route timing-derived frequency from
\studyclaim{StudyTwoOverallSftPenalized} to
\studyclaim{StudyTwoOverallRfPenalized}~MHz, with positive effects for both
policy-training seeds.  The gain reflects faster oracle-passing draws rather
than higher correctness.  Original-MLP and single-seed correctness-only controls
constrain the explanation without isolating individual repair components.

The improvement is neither universal across families nor free of cost.  Higher
LUT/register use, slightly higher vectorless power, and a small matched
VerilogEval regression accompany the physical gain.  Selected setup-closure
and board checks add directional evidence but do not replace the sealed
endpoint or establish full timing sign-off.  The resulting CAD lesson is
specific: a learned proxy can guide a useful distribution shift, but functional
testing, complete failure accounting, and independent physical evaluation must
remain separate from the training objective.
