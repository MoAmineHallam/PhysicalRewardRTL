\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{RTL generation and functional feedback}
VerilogEval and RTLLM established executable evaluation for natural-language to
RTL generation~\cite{liu2023verilogeval,lu2024rtllm}.  VeriGen, RTLCoder, CodeV,
and CraftRTL improve supervised generation or data construction
~\cite{thakur2024verigen,liu2024rtlcoder,zhao2024codev,liu2024craftrtl}.
VeriSeek and VeriReason use verification or executable feedback to improve
reasoning and correctness~\cite{wang2024veriseek,wang2025verireason}.  Our functional oracle
serves the same essential role, but the learning question is orthogonal:
correct RTL remains admissible only, and real physical quality ranks the
admissible implementations.

\paragraph{Physical optimization of generated RTL}
RTLRewriter optimizes existing RTL through rewriting~\cite{yao2024rtlrewriter},
while PPA-RTL and ChipSeek-R1 incorporate synthesis-derived PPA into
reinforcement learning~\cite{ppartl2025,chipseek2025}.  Other evolutionary and
agentic systems search for power, performance, or area at inference time
~\cite{min2025revolution,poet2026,evolve2026,coevo2026,drrtl2026}.
Alpha-RTL instead updates the policy at test time against per-design EDA
feedback~\cite{zhou2026alphartl}.
RTL-OPT contributes a physically evaluated optimization benchmark
~\cite{lu2026rtlopt}.  Our setting instead asks whether
training changes a generator's one-draw distribution, reducing dependence on
repeated generation and physical reranking.  The perfect-selector analysis is
deliberately favorable to inference-time search: it assumes the true post-route
frequency of every supervised draw is known before selection.

\paragraph{Learned rewards and physical predictors}
Reward-model optimization can exploit errors that appear small on the behavior
distribution but grow where an optimizer moves probability
~\cite{gao2023scaling}.  Pre-synthesis predictors remain useful because detailed
implementation is expensive
~\cite{fang2024presynthesis,ustun2020accurate,wu2022highlevel}.  We use such a
predictor only inside training and reconstruct evaluation from Vivado.  The
late-trajectory analysis is consequently a measured instance of proxy
overoptimization, not a claim that every learned physical reward fails.

\paragraph{Positioning}
The paper combines elements that prior lines usually separate: executable
functional gating during policy training, distribution-level physical
evaluation on a frozen parameter split, and a direct comparison between reward
trajectory and post-route reality.  Its scope is narrower than general RTL
generation or arbitrary RTL-to-RTL optimization, but the narrowness makes an
equal-sample physical endpoint and candidate-level mechanism auditable.
