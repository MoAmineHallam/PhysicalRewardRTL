Write a Verilog module named `fir16_8b`, a 16-tap direct-form FIR filter. It has inputs `clk`, active-low `rst_n`, and an 8-bit unsigned sample input `x`, and a 16-bit registered output `y`. Maintain a 16-element tapped delay line of past samples (newest = current x). Each cycle compute the sum of products of the delay-line taps with the fixed 8-bit coefficients {3,7,12,19,27,34,40,43,43,40,34,27,19,12,7,3} and register the low 16 bits of that sum into `y`. On `!rst_n` clear the delay line and `y` to 0.
